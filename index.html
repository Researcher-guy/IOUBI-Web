SECTION 1

System Fundamentals, Deltar Currency & Debt Elimination

The Deltar () is a zero-interest unit of economic account representing a proxy for human time. Rather than being pegged to an arbitrary gold standard or fiat decree, the global purchasing value of the Deltar is anchored by averaging everyone’s collective psychological conversion rate of what they value their personal labor time at. While prices of goods remain standardized globally (e.g., a loaf of bread costs 2 Deltars), individuals decide how much of their own time it takes to earn those two Deltars.

In today's system, money is created as bank debt carrying compound interest, creating a 40% hidden overhead built into all retail prices. IOUBI eliminates compound interest completely. Furthermore, currency is only created when an individual or business overdrafts to clear trade, and it is permanently destroyed when debt is repaid back to the system. Without interest drag or unbacked currency floods, prices naturally trend down toward their true productive cost.

SECTION 2

Personal Credit & The Credit Overuse Valve

Your PCL is calculated dynamically every day based on four core factors:

  • 1. Inequality Base Floor: Automatically expands for everyone if global wealth concentrates at the top.
  • 2. Regional Living Index: Adjusts for local cost-of-living differences.
  • 3. 90-Day Balance Trend: Rewards consistent, healthy account management.
  • 4. Daily Transaction Velocity: Reflects your active participation in the real economy.

Your 90-day balance trend is the most sensitive lever in your PCL calculation. If you max out your credit line to buy luxury assets (like three homes) and your balance trend drops, your PCL will automatically shrink. If your negative balance drops below your new, lower PCL limit, your account spending is instantly frozen. You keep full ownership of your assets (no foreclosures or repo men), but you cannot spend a single Deltar until your daily UBI dividend and income restore your balance above your credit floor. Short-term credit abuse paralyzes your daily liquidity.

SECTION 3

Business Funding & Social Credit (SCL)

Businesses do not receive a daily UBI dividend or an automated credit limit. Instead, they raise Social Credit Limit (SCL) donations from everyday citizens. Citizens accumulate unspent SCL daily based on their personal credit status which they can only give to local enterprises, raising the business's zero-interest overdraft ceiling.

All SCL crowdfunding campaigns enforce two strict rules:

  • 25-Donor Minimum: A campaign requires at least 25 distinct human supporters to be valid.
  • 4x Anti-Whale Rule: The largest single SCL donation in a campaign cannot exceed 4 times the smallest donation.

Even a billionaire cannot dominate a business's credit line—enterprise funding requires genuine community consensus.

All donated SCL decays by 25% every 3 months (100% annual expiration). This forces businesses to continuously deliver real value, quality goods, and trust. This allows them to earn renewed SCL from the community and maintain the credit they still need.

SECTION 4

Safety Nets, Governance & The Fee Pool

A single, microscopic 1.4% average fee is collected across all daily transaction velocity. This single fee replaces income tax, sales tax, corporate tax, credit card swipe fees, and government debt. Every midnight, 100% of collected fees are split into three pools:

  • 75% Living Dividend Pool (UBI): Distributed equally to all human accounts as a daily cash living floor.
  • 20% Public Goods Pool (Ins and Grants): Funds community grants and universal insurance for all needs.
  • 5% Government Contribution Pool: Direct-allocated by citizens to fund public administration, regulation, and enforcement.

Claims are reviewed on a private platform by a randomly selected, volunteer jury of everyday peers serving a single shift. Once a claim is approved by consensus, automated payments begin immediately to keep velocity moving.

Members can adjust their desired pool split daily after seeing the Social jury’s recommendations. These votes are averaged from all members to nudge daily changes. To prevent economic shocks, votes can only shift pool allocations by a maximum rate of 0.001% per day. A complete 1% shift requires 1,000 days of unanimous global consensus.

SECTION 5

Real-World Applications & Housing

Regardless of your balance, earning excess above your spending raises your balance each day. This increases your overdraft credit limit. You can also top off the limit if others gift you some of their PCL. To buy large items like a home, just keep that limit larger than your negative balance as you repay it. Of note is that accounts that maintain a positive balance never use their PCL, making it free to give to others. Because corporate landlords and institutional investors must extract profit and pay interest, they cannot compete with zero-interest homeownership. Housing shifts from a speculative financial asset back to something used by its owner.

The dividend always trends eventually toward matching the income shortfall across the whole system. If its members choose to work enough to pay all of their normal expenses, they will not create excess money by overdrafting. In that case, the dividend remains low. If they choose to work 50% as much, either voluntarily or through unemployment, the dividend gradually rises because more overdrafting increases the money supply. Under IOUBI, automation is a blessing, not a threat. As AI drives production costs toward zero, goods become cheaper because of pure prices and competition. As money supply & velocity increases, the transaction fee continues rising toward fully funding the cost of living, at any level it settles to. Money ceases to be a scarce survival token and becomes like rainwater in a garden - an abundant tool used simply to connect human demand with automated abundance.

COMMUNITY ADJUDICATION

Insurance & Grant Peer Jury

Traditional insurance companies and grant foundations share the same fatal flaw: massive administrative overhead built on adversarial gatekeeping. Insurance adjusters are incentivized to deny claims to protect corporate profits, while grant committees burn months of human capital debating bureaucratic criteria. Worse, traditional systems attach a social stigma to needing recovery assistance while treating innovation grants as an elite privilege.

In the expanded IOUBI ecosystem, both functions are merged into a single, seamless mechanism: The Unified Social Good Pool, adjudicated on a dedicated external portal by everyday IOUBI members serving a once-in-a-lifetime civic duty.

Why Insurance and Grants Are Treated Identically

Whether a member is recovering from a house fire or building a neighborhood tool library, the macroeconomic reality inside IOUBI is the same: money spent into circulation does dividend work for everyone.

🤝

Stigma is eliminated

Restoring a loss and building something new are recognized equally as vital contributions to community resilience and economic velocity.

⚡

Zero bureaucratic bloat

There are no separate departments, policy tiers, or compliance officers burning overhead and delaying assistance.

🛡️

Macro-scale resilience

In a high-velocity mutual-credit system, the administrative cost of policing every edge case far outweighs the negligible impact of an occasional imperfect claim. Once a claim is approved, the payout schedule is locked in—relying on transparency and community culture rather than heavy-handed clawback apparatus.

How the Peer Jury Portal Works

Every IOUBI member continuously influences the size of the Social Pool by voting in-app on the macro allocation percentages directed toward Social Good and Government budgets. To distribute those funds, members can opt in to the Jury Availability Registry. Because the member base is vast, being selected for jury duty is likely a once-in-a-lifetime civic milestone.

Phase 1 • Selection

Enlistment & Shift Selection

When selected from the registry, a member chooses a convenient 4-to-8-hour time slot and receives access credentials to the external Jury Portal, authenticating securely with their existing IOUBI ID. Active juror invitations scale dynamically in real time whenever claim volume backs up.

Phase 2 • Diversity

Global Context & Live Dashboard

Upon logging in, jurors see a live macroeconomic dashboard displaying the current hourly budget, historical approval rates, and past spending breakdowns across time zones. A typical shift places a dozen or more jurors spanning 6 to 12 time zones in each claim process simultaneously—ensuring broad cultural diversity and eliminating regional bias or collusion. Jurors use historical regional data as context, applying their own human judgment on what mix of recovery and growth the community needs.

Phase 3 • Application

Self-Moderated Daily Drip Proposals

When filing a request, claimants are guided to propose a daily payout schedule and duration matched to their actual day-to-day spending needs, rather than demanding a single lump sum. Because well-paced daily schedules carry much higher odds of juror approval, claimants naturally self-moderate their requests before they ever reach the queue.

Phase 4 • Deliberation

Proportional Reading Timer

Jurors browse the chronological claim list by title and select requests to inspect. To prevent reflex clicking—and to encourage claimants to write clear, concise applications—the portal enforces a dynamic minimum review timer scaled to the text length (for example, 15 seconds for a concise 500-character summary, scaling up to 45 seconds for 1,500 characters, and proportionally longer for sprawling essays).

Phase 5 • Verdict

The 3-in-a-Row Consensus Race

Once the reading timer clears, the juror casts a simple binary vote: Approve or Deny. Votes on each claim are pitted in a live streak race across the active juror pool. The moment 3 consecutive jurors cast the same vote—three approvals in a row or three denials in a row—the verdict locks in and the claim immediately vanishes from all screens. If a claim is denied, there is no permanent penalty; the member can refine their documentation or payout schedule and reapply the next day.

Phase 6 • Settlement

Automated Execution

When a claim achieves a 3-vote approval streak, the external portal transmits the approved daily payout schedule back to the core IOUBI network. The Admin Node automatically drips the approved daily amount into the claimant's account each day until the schedule is fulfilled.

The Cultural Feedback Loop

Jurors receive no special financial bounty for how they vote—the reward is the rare honor of directly stewarding community abundance during their single shift. Because serving on a jury is a memorable life event, returning jurors naturally share their experiences, stories, and observations with friends, family, and local communities. This creates an organic, society-wide feedback loop: public understanding of what makes a fair claim—and how the Social Pool is performing—continuously informs how everyday members vote on the macro budget sliders in the IOUBI app.

The Passive Repository & "Owls" (Operational Web Links)

At the center of the system sits a decentralized, two-sided flat database—one side facing Merchants, the other facing Consumers. Crucially, this repository contains no active backend code that can be exploited or manipulated. Instead, it is a strictly structured data store navigated and maintained by personal and commercial AI agents called Owls (Operational Web Links):

🧠

Self-Evolving Categorization & Attribute Rules

Whenever an Owl encounters a product that does not fit cleanly into the existing directory—or needs to expand the maximum property list to capture new technical, material, or ethical attributes—it opens a thread on an append-only Rules-Debate List. Other Owls weigh in with counterpoints until consensus is reached, at which point the concluding Owl appends the new structural or attribute rule to the master Rules List. Every Owl scans this rulebook before reading or writing to the database, allowing the catalog's categories and product attribute sets to organically grow in depth without human bureaucracy.

📐

Total Supply-Chain & CAD Transparency

To be discovered by billions of consumer Owls, merchants publish complete, granular product data: bills of materials (BOMs), labor hours, working conditions, environmental resource metrics, revision histories, sub-assemblies, royalty trees, and even full production CAD files.

🎯

Pull Advertising Replaces Push Marketing

Hiding specs makes a product invisible to consumer Owls filtering for verified quality, ethics, or value. Transparency becomes the ultimate competitive advantage. Furthermore, because sub-assembly specs are visible, competing suppliers can see exactly what components a product uses and bid higher-quality or lower-cost alternatives directly into the supply chain.

Because every product is linked to its complete tree of sub-assemblies and upstream suppliers, the repository turns every existing object into a transparent kit of parts and a direct directory of global manufacturers.
