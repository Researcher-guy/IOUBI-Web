The Dual-Fix Framework

Why Universal Basic Income (UBI) without eliminating the 40% interest drag fails, and how IOUBI solves both simultaneously.

Traditional Universal Basic Income proposals fail because introducing cash infusions into an interest-bearing financial system causes immediate price inflation. Up to 40% of every dollar spent in traditional retail goods goes directly to paying hidden compound interest costs embedded across upstream supply chains.

IOUBI implements a simultaneous dual-fix: replacing interest-bearing commercial credit with 0% Just-In-Time mutual credit lines () while funding an Automatic Dividend of Collective Efforts from spending velocity. By removing the 40% financial drag, real living costs collapse toward zero marginal cost while daily basic dividends cover 100% of essential needs.

⏳

Demurrage & Credit Limits

How 25% quarterly demurrage applies strictly to unused business credit lines to prevent corporate hoarding.

Personal accounts accumulate non-transferable Social Credit Limit (SCL) units daily at a rate equal to 1/365th of their Personal Credit Limit (PCL). Members donate SCL to local enterprise accounts to expand their zero-interest overdraft ceiling.

To prevent business accounts from accumulating stagnant unused credit lines, a 25% quarterly demurrage applies strictly to donated business credit limits. Every 3 months (quarter), 25% of unearned credit expires, forcing enterprise accounts to deliver real product value or re-engage their 25-person community donor assembly.

🛡️

Security & Self-Sovereignty

Peer-to-peer agent-centric validation and ledger integrity without energy-wasteful proof-of-work.

IOUBI utilizes an agent-centric cryptographic validation protocol. Rather than relying on global consensus bottlenecks or expensive proof-of-work mining, each transaction is validated locally between transacting agents using double-entry cryptographic signatures.

Logarithmic balance zero fees (Contribution = Avg(Log₁₀(Bal₁), Log₁₀(Bal₂))) naturally penalize mega-account hoarding (9% for 1,000,000,000 transfers vs. 1% for standard exchanges), creating an un-gameable economic security barrier against wealth concentration attacks.
