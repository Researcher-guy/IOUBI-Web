CORE SPECIFICATIONS

The Five Fundamental Accounting Mechanics

The IOUBI system replaces traditional debt-backed currency with five straightforward accounting mechanics that maintain economic stability, eliminate artificial inflation, and guarantee zero-interest balance across society.

01

The Daily Living Dividend (UBI)

👤 Personal Perspective

Every morning, a small dividend payout arrives in your account based on yesterday’s overall economic activity—no applications, qualifications, or repayments required.

This dividend naturally trends toward closing whatever gap remains to cover the cost of living chosen by its members, with the speed of that closing depending directly on system usage. This creates a permanent UBI (Universal Basic Income) floor under your life so basic daily needs are always met, regardless of your employment status.

⚡ Economic Engine

A non-tax-funded distribution mechanism powered directly by real-time transaction velocity. Fee revenues from daily circulation are pooled and re-allocated per capita every 24 hours, permanently stabilizing baseline aggregate demand across the macroeconomy without government debt or currency dilution.

02

Overdraft Flexibility & How Money Moves

👤 Personal Perspective

You can spend using positive savings or by simply overdrafting your account up to an interest-free limit.

How it works in practice:
  • When Sarah spends 100 for groceries using an overdraft, the market receives 100 in spendable money that is identical to funds drawn from savings. Her next 100 in earnings automatically repays that debt first before her balance can rise above zero into positive savings.
  • The Takeaway: Spending lowers your balance (even into an overdraft), and receiving money raises it. Account balances reflect net savings or borrowing, while money spent is simply the record of those changes.
  • No Speculation: Because credit carries zero interest and an account must clear its negative balance before net gains can accumulate, predatory lending and price speculation disappear. Prices stay naturally stable because money cannot be saved as profit until the underlying debt that created it is paid.
⚡ Economic Engine

A zero-interest mutual credit clearing framework where aggregate liabilities continuously equal aggregate assets (∑ Balances = 0).

The total money supply naturally matches real economic need—plus any temporarily stagnant savings—without generating artificial inflation, while progressive fee rates discourage rising or excessively large balances. Individual overdraft limits are calibrated to reflect real-world purchasing capacity, ensuring access to everyday goods and major purchases without debt traps.

03

Crowdsourced Business Credit & Social Credit

👤 Personal Perspective

Anyone can enable one business account, but getting a commercial credit line doesn't involve bank loan officers. Instead, a business account only receives credit when approved by a crowd of at least 25 people.

Every individual automatically receives a yearly pool of "Social Credit" matched to their personal credit limit. Social Credit is completely free to build and free to gift, but it can only be given to businesses you want to support for startups, growth, or daily operations. Like personal accounts, a business doesn't draw on its credit line as long as its balance stays positive. Because gifted Social Credit expires in 25% increments over the course of a year, businesses must continuously earn community approval to maintain their zero-interest credit.

⚡ Economic Engine

A decentralized credit-underwriting mechanism that replaces private banking gatekeepers with community-driven risk allocation. By requiring a minimum threshold of 25 distinct individual endorsers, commercial credit expansion is decoupled from centralized collateral demands.

Social Credit operates as a dedicated, non-transferable token issued annually per capita and pegged to personal credit allowances. Pledged Social Credit establishes a zero-interest overdraft ceiling for the target enterprise without drawing down the endorser's personal balances. An automatic annual decay rate (25% per quarter) enforces dynamic credit recalibration, preventing dormant entities from hoarding credit capacity while matching money creation directly to active community utility.

04

The Universal Progressive Transaction Fee

👤 Personal Perspective

With every payment, a small fee (typically around 1%) goes into the pool for the UBI dividend and other social costs. This small cut on overall spending fully funds the universal safety nets without income taxes, sales taxes, or bank interest.

If an account holds a very large positive balance, the fee rate scales up slightly—encouraging money to flow back into circulation rather than sit stagnant.

⚡ Economic Engine

A progressive velocity levy assessed on transaction volume, where the fee percentage programmatically scales based on the number of places (digits) in an account's positive balance.

Zero-interest credit, carrying no financing penalties, encourages outright payment over delayed financing, yielding instant settlement and equity transfer. This replaces profit-from-debt business models with direct market competition based on product quality and real value.

05

Universal Insurance & Systemic Renewal

👤 Personal Perspective

You automatically receive universal insurance for needs of any size. Claims can cover unexpected personal losses like traditional insurance or fund grants for local community projects.

Instead of facing debt collectors or ruined credit during hardship, shared protection keeps buyers and sellers whole while encouraging community initiative.

⚡ Economic Engine

A built-in, rolling pool funds both insurance and grants through a just-in-time payment mechanism.

By rapidly recycling funds back into active transaction velocity, the protocol prevents localized defaults from cascading through supply chains and cancels unintentional economic harm.

THE WHY AND WHAT

The Why and What

Two systemic flaws holding humanity back—and the two zero-debt mechanisms designed to solve them.

DIMENSION 01

The Economic System — Financial Overhead

THE PROBLEM

The Invisible 40% Cost

The Built-In Cost of Debt-Based Money

Currently, most money is created through debt. Banks lend money into existence, and every dollar comes with an obligation to pay back more to cover the interest.

Because businesses, consumers, and governments all pass on this cost for their debt, interest payments siphon off money at every level of society. You pay for this overhead directly out of your paycheck and indirectly inside the price of everything you buy.

Where That 40% Overhead Goes:
  • Embedded Supply Chain Debt (12%–15%): Compounding loan interest built into retail goods at every production step.
  • Personal & Household Debt (10%–12%): Direct interest on mortgages, credit cards, auto loans, and student loans.
  • Government Debt & Taxes (6%–8%): Taxes diverted strictly to debt interest rather than public services.
  • Banking Fees & Overhead (6%–8%): Merchant swipe fees and financial rent-seeking.
THE IOUBI SOLUTION

A Zero-Debt Foundation

Money Created for People, Not Profit

IOUBI replaces debt-based currency with a zero-interest account ledger. People can opt-out of asking banks to lend money into existence with compound interest.

By removing interest-bearing debt, the system restores full purchasing power to every unit () in the economy.

How IOUBI Solves the Overhead:
  • Universal Credit: Fair credit limits scaling dynamically to meet real need.
  • Interest-Free Supply Chains: Eliminates the built-in 12%–15% compounding debt markup.
  • 100% Retained Income: No mortgage or loan interest draining household wealth.
  • Universal Transaction Fee: Microscopic fee pool replaces complex taxes and interest.
DIMENSION 02

The Human Experience — Leverage & Dignity

THE PROBLEM

The Erosion of Human Power

The Loss of Everyday Leverage

When survival depends on constant debt and mandatory labor, individuals lose the power to walk away from bad deals. Without baseline financial security, true fairness and real choice cannot exist.

Where the Imbalance Plays Out:
  • Market Powerlessness: Workers unable to say "no" must accept lower wages and unfair terms.
  • Political Disenfranchisement: Concentrated capital dominates political campaigns and policy.
  • Institutional Disadvantage: Wealthy entities outlast individuals in legal and contractual disputes.
THE IOUBI SOLUTION

Restoring Human Leverage

A Guaranteed Floor on Living Standards

IOUBI restores balance by providing every individual with an unconditional daily living dividend and baseline credit floor. When basic survival is permanently secured, people regain the natural leverage to negotiate and participate as equals.

How IOUBI Restores Power:
  • Labor Leverage: A guaranteed living floor gives creators and workers the real power to say "no."
  • Civic Equality: Concentrated capital cannot override the voices of everyday citizens.
  • Balanced Standing: Universal baseline credit and insurance prevent financial coercion.

SECTION 01

1. Fundamentals & The Deltar Currency

The Deltar () is a zero-interest unit of economic account representing a proxy for human time. Rather than being pegged to an arbitrary gold standard or fiat decree, the global purchasing value of the Deltar is anchored by averaging everyone's collective psychological conversion rate of what they value their personal labor time at.

While prices of goods remain standardized globally (e.g., a loaf of bread costs 2), individuals decide how much of their own time it takes to earn those two Deltars.

In today's system, money is created as bank debt carrying compound interest, creating a 40% hidden overhead built into all retail prices. IOUBI eliminates compound interest completely.

Furthermore, currency is only created when an individual or business overdrafts to clear trade, and it is permanently destroyed when debt is repaid back to the system. Without interest drag or unbacked currency floods, prices naturally trend down toward their true productive cost.

SECTION 02

2. Personal Credit & The Credit Overuse Valve

Your PCL is calculated dynamically every day based on four core factors:

  1. Inequality Base Floor: Automatically expands for everyone if global wealth concentrates at the top.
  2. Regional Living Index: Adjusts for local cost-of-living differences.
  3. 90-Day Balance Trend: Rewards consistent, healthy account management.
  4. Daily Transaction Velocity: Reflects your active participation in the real economy.

Your 90-day balance trend is the most sensitive lever in your PCL calculation. If you max out your credit line to buy luxury assets (like three homes) and your balance trend drops, your PCL will automatically shrink.

If your negative balance drops below your new, lower PCL limit, your account spending is instantly frozen. You keep full ownership of your assets (no foreclosures or repo men), but you cannot spend a single Deltar until your daily UBI dividend and income restore your balance above your credit floor. Short-term credit abuse paralyzes your daily liquidity.

SECTION 03

3. Business Funding & Social Credit (SCL)

Businesses do not receive a daily UBI dividend or an automated credit limit. Instead, they raise Social Credit Limit (SCL) donations from everyday citizens. Citizens accumulate unspent SCL daily based on their personal credit status which they can only give to local enterprises, raising the business's zero-interest overdraft ceiling.

All SCL crowdfunding campaigns enforce two strict rules:

  • 25-Donor Minimum: A campaign requires at least 25 distinct human supporters to be valid.
  • 4x Anti-Whale Rule: The largest single SCL donation in a campaign cannot exceed 4 times the smallest donation.

Even a billionaire cannot dominate a business's credit line—enterprise funding requires genuine community consensus.

All donated SCL decays by 25% every 3 months (100% annual expiration). This forces businesses to continuously deliver real value, quality goods, and trust. This allows them to earn renewed SCL from the community and maintain the credit they still need.

SECTION 04

4. Safety Nets, Governance & The Fee Pool

A single, microscopic 1.4% average fee is collected across all daily transaction velocity. This single fee replaces income tax, sales tax, corporate tax, credit card swipe fees, and government debt.

Every midnight, 100% of collected fees are split into three pools:

  • 75% UBI Dividend Pool: Distributed equally to all human accounts as a daily cash living floor.
  • 20% Social Pool: Funds community grants and universal insurance for all needs.
  • 5% Government Pool: Direct-allocated by citizens to fund public administration, regulation, and enforcement.

Claims are reviewed on a private platform by a randomly selected, volunteer jury of everyday peers serving a single shift. Once a claim is approved by consensus, automated payments begin immediately to keep velocity moving.

Members can adjust their desired pool split daily after seeing the Social jury’s recommendations. These votes are averaged from all members to nudge daily changes. To prevent economic shocks, votes can only shift pool allocations by a maximum rate of 0.001% per day. A complete 1% shift requires 1,000 days of unanimous global consensus.

SECTION 05

5. Real-World Applications & Housing

Regardless of your balance, earning and saving both raise your overdraft credit limit. To buy large items like a home, just keep that limit higher than your negative balance as you repay it. Because corporate landlords and institutional investors must extract high profit margins and pay interest, they cannot compete with zero-interest homeownership. Housing shifts from a speculative financial asset back to something used by its owner.

Under IOUBI, automation is a blessing, not a threat. As AI drives production costs toward zero, goods become cheaper because of pure prices and competition. As money velocity increases, the transaction fee continues rising toward fully funding the cost of living. Money ceases to be a scarce survival token and becomes like rainwater in a garden—an abundant tool used simply to connect human demand with automated abundance.
