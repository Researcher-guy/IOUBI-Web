CORE SPECIFICATIONS

The Five Fundamental Accounting Mechanics

The IOUBI system replaces traditional debt-backed currency with five straightforward accounting mechanics that maintain economic stability, eliminate artificial inflation, and guarantee zero-interest balance across society.

01

The Daily Living Dividend (UBI)

👤 Personal Perspective

Every morning, a small dividend payout arrives in your account based on yesterday’s overall economic activity—no applications, qualifications, or repayments required.

This dividend naturally trends toward closing whatever gap remains to cover the cost of living chosen by its members, with the speed of that closing depending directly on system usage. This creates a permanent UBI (Universal Basic Income) floor under your life so basic daily needs are always met, regardless of your employment status.

⚡ Economic Engine

A non-tax-funded distribution mechanism powered directly by real-time transaction velocity. Fee revenues from daily circulation are pooled and re-allocated per capita every 24 hours, permanently stabilizing baseline aggregate demand across the macroeconomy without government debt or currency dilution.

02

Overdraft Flexibility & How Money Moves

👤 Personal Perspective

You can spend using positive savings or by simply overdrafting your account up to an interest-free limit.

How it works in practice:
  • When Sarah spends 100 for groceries using an overdraft, the market receives 100 in spendable money that is identical to funds drawn from savings. Her next 100 in earnings automatically repays that debt first before her balance can rise above zero into positive savings.
  • The Takeaway: Spending lowers your balance (even into an overdraft), and receiving money raises it. Account balances reflect net savings or borrowing, while money spent is simply the record of those changes.
  • No Speculation: Because credit carries zero interest and an account must clear its negative balance before net gains can accumulate, predatory lending and price speculation disappear. Prices stay naturally stable because money cannot be saved as profit until the underlying debt that created it is paid.
⚡ Economic Engine

A zero-interest mutual credit clearing framework where aggregate liabilities continuously equal aggregate assets (∑ Balances = 0).

The total money supply naturally matches real economic need—plus any temporarily stagnant savings—without generating artificial inflation, while progressive fee rates discourage rising or excessively large balances. Individual overdraft limits are calibrated to reflect real-world purchasing capacity, ensuring access to everyday goods and major purchases without debt traps.

03

Crowdsourced Business Credit & Social Credit

👤 Personal Perspective

Anyone can enable one business account, but getting a commercial credit line doesn't involve bank loan officers. Instead, a business account only receives credit when approved by a crowd of at least 25 people.

Every individual automatically receives a yearly pool of "Social Credit" matched to their personal credit limit. Social Credit is completely free to build and free to gift, but it can only be given to businesses you want to support for startups, growth, or daily operations. Like personal accounts, a business doesn't draw on its credit line as long as its balance stays positive. Because gifted Social Credit expires in 25% increments over the course of a year, businesses must continuously earn community approval to maintain their zero-interest credit.

⚡ Economic Engine

A decentralized credit-underwriting mechanism that replaces private banking gatekeepers with community-driven risk allocation. By requiring a minimum threshold of 25 distinct individual endorsers, commercial credit expansion is decoupled from centralized collateral demands.

Social Credit operates as a dedicated, non-transferable token issued annually per capita and pegged to personal credit allowances. Pledged Social Credit establishes a zero-interest overdraft ceiling for the target enterprise without drawing down the endorser's personal balances. An automatic annual decay rate (25% per quarter) enforces dynamic credit recalibration, preventing dormant entities from hoarding credit capacity while matching money creation directly to active community utility.

04

The Universal Progressive Transaction Fee

👤 Personal Perspective

With every payment, a small fee (typically around 1%) goes into the pool for the UBI dividend and other social costs. This small cut on overall spending fully funds the universal safety nets without income taxes, sales taxes, or bank interest.

If an account holds a very large positive balance, the fee rate scales up slightly—encouraging money to flow back into circulation rather than sit stagnant.

⚡ Economic Engine

A progressive velocity levy assessed on transaction volume, where the fee percentage programmatically scales based on the number of places (digits) in an account's positive balance.

Zero-interest credit, carrying no financing penalties, encourages outright payment over delayed financing, yielding instant settlement and equity transfer. This replaces profit-from-debt business models with direct market competition based on product quality and real value.

05

Universal Insurance & Systemic Renewal

👤 Personal Perspective

You automatically receive universal insurance for needs of any size. Claims can cover unexpected personal losses like traditional insurance or fund grants for local community projects.

Instead of facing debt collectors or ruined credit during hardship, shared protection keeps buyers and sellers whole while encouraging community initiative.

⚡ Economic Engine

A built-in, rolling pool funds both insurance and grants through a just-in-time payment mechanism.

By rapidly recycling funds back into active transaction velocity, the protocol prevents localized defaults from cascading through supply chains and cancels unintentional economic harm.

DIMENSION 01

The Economic System — Financial Overhead

THE PROBLEM

The Invisible 40% Cost

The Built-In Cost of Debt-Based Money

Currently, most money is created through debt. Banks lend money into existence, and every dollar comes with an obligation to pay back more to cover the interest.

Because businesses, consumers, and governments all pass on this cost for their debt, interest payments siphon off money at every level of society. You pay for this overhead directly out of your paycheck and indirectly inside the price of everything you buy.

Where That 40% Overhead Goes:
  • Embedded Supply Chain Debt (12%–15%): Farmers, factories, shipping companies, and retail stores all take out loans for equipment and inventory. Each business raises its prices to cover its interest payments, compounding the price of goods at every step from raw materials to store shelves.
  • Personal & Household Debt (10%–12%): Direct interest paid on mortgages, credit cards, auto loans, and student debt eats away at take-home pay, reducing the real purchasing power of every paycheck.
  • Government Debt & Taxes (6%–8%): A huge portion of federal, state, and local taxes goes strictly toward paying interest on public debt rather than funding roads, healthcare, or public services.
  • Banking Fees & Processing Overhead (6%–8%): Credit card swipe fees, merchant transaction fees, loan underwriting costs, and banking profit margins add a final tax to every purchase.
The Hidden Drag on Life:

By the time you pay taxes, cover your personal debt, and buy basic goods, roughly 40% of the total wealth created in the economy goes strictly toward servicing debt and banking overhead—rather than paying for actual physical goods, quality work, or human well-being.

THE IOUBI SOLUTION

A Zero-Debt Foundation

Money Created for People, Not Profit

IOUBI replaces debt-based currency with a zero-interest account ledger. People can opt-out of asking banks to lend money into existence with compound interest. IOUBI administers the credit directly to individuals and businesses so society can lend directly to them without interest.

By removing interest-bearing debt, including middleman extraction, the system restores the full and pure purchasing power to every unit () in the economy.

How IOUBI Solves the Overhead:
  • Universal Credit: Everyone receives a fair credit limit that’s not centrally controlled by the motives of any group. It scales to meet any need, large and small.
  • Interest-Free Supply Chains: Farmers, manufacturers, and shippers operate without compound interest payments, eliminating the hidden 12%–15% inflation built into everyday goods.
  • 100% Retained Income: With no personal mortgages, auto debt, or credit card interest eating away at your paycheck, your take-home income goes entirely toward your chosen quality of life.
  • Universal Transaction Fee: A small progressive fee on every transaction in the supply chain funds the entire system. It replaces complex taxes, payment swipe fees, and bank profit margins with a dividend on all economic activity.
The Real-World Result:

By removing financial interest and banking overhead, the cost of living drops while real purchasing power rises, returning the missing 40% of economic value directly to the people who create it.

DIMENSION 02

The Human Experience — Leverage & Dignity

THE PROBLEM

The Erosion of Human Power

The Loss of Everyday Leverage

Under our current economic rules, money is more than a medium of exchange—it is power. As wealth concentrates at the top, it is used to win and to increase the chances of winning. Everyday people lose the leverage they need to negotiate fairly in their jobs, their communities, and their government.

When survival depends on constant debt and mandatory labor, individuals lose the power to walk away from bad deals. Without baseline financial security, true fairness and real choice cannot exist.

Where the Imbalance Plays Out:
  • Market & Labor Powerlessness: Without a guaranteed safety net, workers and small producers cannot say "no." When walking away isn't an option, corporations dictate lower wages, worse working conditions, and unfair prices.
  • Political Disenfranchisement: Concentrated capital dominates political campaigns, lobbying, and policy-making. As money dictates political outcomes, the average citizen's vote carries far less weight than big capital.
  • Social & Systemic Inequality: Essential human needs—housing, healthcare, and education—become financial leverage points used against people, trapping individuals in an endless cycle of survival mode.
  • Institutional Advantage for Capital: Large institutions and wealthy actors use deep financial reserves to outlast individuals in legal, regulatory, and contractual disputes, ensuring the rules stay stacked in their favor.
The Loss of Real Freedom:

By stripping people of baseline economic independence, the system removes their negotiating power across society, forcing millions to accept unfair terms just to survive.

THE IOUBI SOLUTION

Restoring Human Leverage

A Guaranteed Floor on Living Standards

IOUBI restores balance by providing every individual with an unconditional daily living dividend and baseline credit floor. When basic survival is permanently secured, people regain the natural leverage to negotiate, participate, and make choices on equal footing.

True freedom exists only when fair competition cannot be removed. This ensures that the rules can remain honest and equal.

How IOUBI Restores Power:
  • Market & Labor Leverage: An unconditional living floor gives workers, creators, and small producers the power to say "no." When walking away is a real option, wages, working conditions, and trade terms must work for both parties.
  • Political & Civic Equality: By separating human dignity from wealth accumulation, individual voice is restored—preventing concentrated capital from overriding the needs, voices, and votes of everyday citizens.
  • Social Security & Dignity: Guaranteed access to daily living resources permanently removes the fear of poverty, shifting society from constant survival mode to stability, passion, and purpose.
  • Balanced Institutional Standing: Universal baseline credit and insurance ensure that regular people can stand up for their rights in legal, social, and contractual disputes without being financially outlasted.
  • Unites Divided Groups Globally: Without permanent lower economic status, communities across nations can collaborate as equals on their true economic potential.
Power Restored to the Individual:

By guaranteeing an unconditional living floor for every human being, IOUBI returns real negotiating power to the person, ensuring that all market, social, and political interactions occur among equals.
