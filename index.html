SECTION 1

System Fundamentals, Deltar Currency & Debt Elimination

IOUBI (Universal Basic Income via Mutual Credit) is a decentralized, zero-interest monetary system that replaces debt-backed fiat currency with an algorithmic mutual credit ledger. In the traditional banking system, money is loaned into existence with compound interest attached—meaning more debt always exists than there is money to repay it. IOUBI eliminates private bank credit creation entirely. Money exists as an accounting unit (the Deltar) created at the point of exchange through zero-interest personal overdraft limits and settled via a dynamic velocity fee.

The Deltar () is the native closed-system numeraire of the IOUBI protocol. Unlike fiat currencies that inflate endlessly, the Deltar is pegged to the global normalized cost of one hour of median human labor and essential living needs. Because money supply dynamically expands and contracts strictly with trade volume (Just-In-Time clearing), the Deltar maintains absolute purchasing power parity without inflation or deflation.

Inflation in fiat systems is caused by expanding the money supply faster than the production of real goods and services, combined with interest tribute. In IOUBI, no unbacked money is ever printed. Money is issued strictly as a temporary negative balance during a legitimate transaction and is destroyed as accounts return toward zero through trade. When trade speeds up, velocity handles the volume rather than inflating the permanent money stock.

SECTION 2

Personal Credit Limits (PCL), Spending Trend & Activity

Your Personal Credit Limit is governed by an objective mathematical formula:

PCL = (BaseCL × R) + (BaseCL × Trend × Activity)

  • BaseCL: The global baseline credit floor, dynamically adjusted to offset systemic inequality.
  • R (Regional Cost Modifier): Adjusts the floor for local cost of living ($2/day = 0.20x, $5/day = 1.00x, $10/day = 2.00x).
  • Trend: Tracks your recent median balance trajectory (1.0x to 100.0x), rewarding steady earnings and savings.
  • Activity: A multiplier (1.0x to 2.0x) based on your daily transaction frequency, incentivizing active circulation.

Personal Credit Limits (PCL) belong exclusively to verified individuals for personal living needs and earn dynamic growth based on activity and savings. Social Credit Limits (SCL), by contrast, represent the community-allocated credit capacity of businesses and enterprises. Businesses never receive daily automated UBI or automated credit limits. Instead, businesses must earn their credit capacity through voluntary community SCL donations from real customers who trust them.

SECTION 3

Business Crowdfunding, Anti-Whale Rules & SCL Decay

Enterprises raise capital via Decentralized SCL Crowdfunding. Citizens allocate unused portions of their SCL accrual to businesses they support. To prevent monopolistic capture, all crowdfunding campaigns must comply with strict Anti-Whale Rules:

  • Minimum Backers: At least 25 unique verified individuals must back the enterprise.
  • Spread Limit: The largest individual gift (D_max) cannot exceed 4 times the smallest gift (D_min).

All enterprise Social Credit Limits undergo an automatic 25% quarterly decay (reducing by 25% every 3 months). This mechanic prevents defunct corporations or bad actors from sitting on massive idle credit lines forever. To maintain its credit ceiling over time, an enterprise must continuously deliver real value, quality goods, and responsive service to earn periodic renewals from its community backer base.

SECTION 4

Velocity Fees, Living Dividend Pools & Governance

Instead of flat sales taxes, IOUBI uses a logarithmic balance fee:

Fee % = average(log10(max(10, Bal_A)), log10(max(10, Bal_B))) / 100

A transaction between small everyday balances carries a tiny ~1% fee, while transactions between massive corporate balances scale up to 4–5%. This creates a natural anti-hoarding pressure without requiring audits or IRS bureaucracy.

100% of fees are routed to public utility smart contracts:

  • 50% — Living Dividend Pool: Disbursed daily to every verified citizen, guaranteeing basic living security.
  • 50% — Public Goods & Infrastructure Pool: Allocated transparently by democratic citizen voting and peer juries for healthcare, education, transit, and ecological regeneration.

Pool distributions are governed by continuous daily citizen voting based on Social Jury recommendations. To ensure economic stability, voting results can shift allocations by a maximum rate of 0.001% per day (requiring 1,000 continuous days of supermajority voting to move an allocation by 1%). This prevents sudden budget shocks while remaining fully responsive to long-term public priorities.

SECTION 5

Real-World Implementation, Housing & Technology

In IOUBI, earning and saving both raise overdraft limits for primary homeownership without thirty-year compound interest mortgages. Because homes are purchased through zero-interest mutual credit rather than leveraged bank debt, housing costs decrease by over 50%. Speculative real estate hoarding is eliminated because unused secondary properties face steep progressive velocity holding fees.

Under legacy capitalism, AI-driven automation causes unemployment and economic collapse because workers lose income to buy goods. Under IOUBI, automation lowers production costs while velocity fees on automated commercial transactions fully fund the living dividend pool. Like rainwater circulating through a thriving garden, technological abundance is automatically recycled into universal human purchasing power.
